Your character

Only your character's plain chat (says:) uses the brighter “You” color, so both sides of a conversation are easy to tell apart. Every other line type has one color for everyone. Underscores and spaces both work.

Mouse and keyboard shortcuts
  • Drag the image to move it (or hold Space and drag anywhere, even over the log).
  • Ctrl + scroll (or pinch on a trackpad) zooms around the cursor.
  • Alt + drag on the log moves the log. Floating labels are dragged directly.
  • Click the preview, then arrows move the image (Shift = 10 px) and Alt + arrows move the log.
  • + / - zoom, 1 = 100%, 0 fit in frame, F fill frame, double-click the image = 100%.
  • View zoom (the preview only, not the PNG): the bar above the preview, or Ctrl + 1 = 100%, Ctrl + 0 = fit to screen, Ctrl + +/- zoom, with the preview focused.
  • With a label selected: arrows move it, Delete removes it, Esc deselects.
Floating labels
Floating labels Click a label on the preview to edit it, drag it to move it (arrows too, Delete removes it).
